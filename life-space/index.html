How Life Space works

Move freely

Drag cards by their top edge. Drag empty space to pan. Pinch or use the zoom controls.

Edit naturally

Click text and type. Changes save automatically. Resize a card from its lower corner.

Keep it yours

Your workspace stays available offline. Sign in to sync an encrypted copy across devices, and export a backup whenever you want.

Draw over it

Draw on a card and the sketch moves with it. Draw on empty space and the sketch stays with the world.
