The reset · 6 min read

You don’t need to quit tomorrow.

You need a plan so leaving is a choice, not an emergency.

When a job is draining you, the internet offers two bad options: endure forever or leap dramatically. Most adults have bills, family, health needs, and commitments. The better move is to build an exit runway in public, one small proof at a time.

Start with a number

Write down the monthly amount that would make a transition possible. Include housing, food, insurance, transportation, debt minimums, and a modest buffer. The number is not a judgment. It is a design constraint.

Build one paid path

Choose the smallest offer that uses something you already know how to do. A day of skilled work, a technical diagnostic, a simple website, or a recurring support lane is more useful than a grand business identity.

Don’t ask, “What business should I start?” Ask, “Who already has a problem I can solve this month?”

Run a 30-day test

  • Talk to ten plausible buyers.
  • Make one clear offer with one price.
  • Track replies, conversations, deposits, and repeat interest.
  • Keep your day job while the evidence accumulates.

The goal is not to feel brave. The goal is to reduce uncertainty until the next move is obvious.
