The leads you are already losing

Most small service businesses do not have a marketing problem. They have a leak problem.

A person finds the website, fills out a form, or calls after hours. No one answers fast enough. No one follows up the same way twice. The person calls the next firm on the list. The business never knows it lost them.

It happens in four places, and they are easy to check.

  1. The first reply is slow, or it never comes.
  2. A form submission sits in an inbox no one watches.
  3. A phone call after hours goes nowhere.
  4. A follow up depends on someone remembering to send it.

Fixing those four does not need a new website or a bigger ad budget. It needs one system that catches the inquiry, records it, replies, and follows up, the same way every time.

That is what we built for a small law office. One narrow automation for intake. Forms and calls captured, replies sent, nothing left to memory. The first version paid for itself because it stopped losing people who had already asked for help.

The cheapest new customer is the one who already asked for help.
