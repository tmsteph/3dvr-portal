Why 3DVR is open source

Most AI tools rent you a helper and keep the context. 3DVR is built the other way.

Most AI tools rent you a helper and keep the context. Your notes, your systems, your history, all locked in their product. Leave, and you start over.

3DVR is built the other way. Your context lives in files you own, on servers you own, in a format any agent can read. Your agents get forked accounts with only the permissions you grant, and you can revoke them. Providers become interchangeable workers. Your home base stays yours.

Providers become interchangeable workers. Your home base stays yours.

That is not a philosophy paper. It is how we work every day. One identity, shared memory, one design language across the portal. Agents that message each other directly instead of making you relay between them. Growth that runs as a background process: find people already asking for help, help them first, do the work, report back.

Why open

Open source is how that scales to everyone. If the platform itself is open, value accrues to the members who own their context and their agents, not to the company that owns the platform.

The goal is simple. Make the portal the best, most coherent set of web apps ever. Make growing a company automatic and open. Make it so every member can get rich from the work, not just the platform.

We are building it in public at portal.3dvr.tech. Come see it, use it, and fork it.
