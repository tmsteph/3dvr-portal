Architecture

Open the whole stack, layer by layer.

The vehicle is both a physical machine and a computing system. The project treats both as things an owner should be able to inspect, repair, and improve.

Physical

Mechanical platform

Chassis, suspension, steering, braking, body, mounting standards, and service access with CAD and a real bill of materials.

Power

Energy + propulsion

Document battery modules, BMS, charger, inverter, motor, thermal systems, interfaces, and high-voltage boundaries.

Network

Vehicle communication

Isolated CAN or equivalent networks, gateways, open diagnostics, logging, simulation, and replayable test data.

Compute

Open cockpit

Linux-based navigation, cameras, media, owner-controlled connectivity, local storage, and a 3DVR HUD without mandatory cloud dependence.

Hardware

Modular computer

Replaceable compute rather than a disposable dashboard, with a long-term RISC-V path where the hardware is ready for the role.

Owner layer

3DVR

Maintenance knowledge, trip tools, local AI, automation, and personal context controlled by the person using the vehicle.

Roadmap

Retrofit first. Full platform later.

We can learn on ordinary vehicles without pretending we are ready to redesign every safety-critical system. Each stage should produce useful open artifacts for the next.

1 · CockpitLinux, sensors, GPS, cameras, read-only OBD-II/CAN.
2 · LabSimulation, replayable buses, bench rigs, hardware-in-the-loop.
3 · Prototype EVDocumented donor components and modular vehicle management.
4 · Physical platformOpen CAD, BOM, wiring, fabrication, and service documentation.
5 · Open computePush more of the stack toward auditable Linux and open silicon.

Safety boundary: read before building.

Early work in a road vehicle stays read-only or non-critical. Steering, braking, propulsion, restraints, high-voltage battery controls, and other safety-critical functions require appropriate engineering analysis, fault handling, testing, and regulatory review before real-world deployment. Active-control experiments belong first in simulation, bench rigs, RC-scale platforms, or isolated test vehicles.
