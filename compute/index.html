Roadmap

Build the open layers one at a time.

We are not waiting for a perfectly open computer. We start with hardware that exists, learn from it, and replace closed layers as the community gains capability.

1. PrototypeFit real modular boards into a practical laptop architecture and validate power, display, input, storage, thermals, and Linux.
2. Open modulesPublish mechanical, electrical, firmware, and service documentation with clear interfaces between modules.
3. Open supply chainTrace components, find repairable substitutes, and work with community and regional manufacturers.
4. Open ecosystemSupport new RISC-V boards, accelerators, accessories, and future community-designed compute hardware.
