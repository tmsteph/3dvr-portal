3DVR Open Source Everyday Life · Core Concept

A portable room in backpack form.

The backpack is not just a container. Its structure becomes the furniture and infrastructure you need when you arrive: standing desk, clothing rack, gear stand, and modular workstation.

Build the first prototype Back to Everyday Life

Design Thesis

Carry the furniture inside the object.

Most bags stop being useful when you arrive. Then the laptop needs a desk, clothing needs a rack, equipment needs a stand, and accessories spread across whatever surface is available. The Portable Room Backpack treats the frame itself as reusable infrastructure.

Core Modes

One frame, many rooms.

Backpack

Carries daily equipment, clothing, computer, tools, food, and personal items.

Standing desk

Telescoping structure supports a laptop, keyboard, tablet, or portable display at working height.

Clothing rack

Uprights extend above the bag and lock into a crossbar for jackets, work clothes, costumes, or laundry.

Gear stand

Mounting points organize headphones, cables, cameras, AV equipment, VR gear, and tools.

Mobile workstation

Standard attachment points let work surfaces and accessories snap onto the same structural platform.

Personal basecamp

Future fabric, power, lighting, privacy, seating, and shelter modules turn the system into a tiny portable room.

Mechanical Platform

The frame is the product.

The bag is one skin around a durable open frame. A practical first architecture uses lightweight telescoping rails, locking hinges, adjustable feet, removable crossbars, and standardized mounting points. Soft goods can change without throwing away the structure.

Design Principles

Open personal infrastructure.

TransformativeEvery structural part should do more than one useful job.
RepairableFasteners, rails, fabric panels, straps, and modules can be replaced independently.
Human scaleUseful on a sidewalk, backstage, hotel room, train platform, campsite, or small apartment.
ModularPublish attachment dimensions so other people can design compatible modules.
Stable firstA desk or rack must be mechanically trustworthy before adding clever electronics.
Open by defaultCAD, dimensions, materials, load tests, failures, repairs, and variants belong in the public recipe.

Prototype Path

Start with the transformation that matters.

01 · Frame

Build a stable wearable frame with two telescoping uprights and fold-out feet.

02 · Clothing rack

Add a removable crossbar and prove that the deployed frame can hold real garments safely.

03 · Standing desk

Add an adjustable work surface sized for a laptop and compact keyboard, then test height and stability.

04 · Open interface

Define mounting-hole spacing, load targets, replacement parts, and a simple module specification.
