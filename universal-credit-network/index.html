Universal Credit Network

A simple idea: what if people could keep transparent, verifiable records of what they owe each other, without needing a cryptocurrency?

Research prototype only. Not a bank, wallet, payment service, cryptocurrency, or real-money credit product. Do not use it to create or settle real obligations.

How it could work

1. Create an IOU

Record who owes whom, the amount in USD cents, a due date, and a digest of the agreed terms.

2. Agree and track

Record acceptance, disputes, and changes as ordered events.

3. Close the record

Eventually verify settlement and preserve a clear history. Real payment verification is not implemented.

Where we are

Early TypeScript protocol and reducer work is underway. Identity authorization, signatures, event ordering, privacy, synchronization, tests, and regulatory review need more work. Nothing here is ready for financial use.

Build with us

We welcome review of the data model, security assumptions, and practical use cases. Follow the project issue · Read the design notes · Contribute on GitHub.
