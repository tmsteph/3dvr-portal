Organizing Human Potential

What if the organizational power of a business were accessible to everyone?

I've been thinking about how businesses are essentially concentrated human effort.

A person only has so many hours in a week. But a business can combine the time, knowledge, tools, and resources of hundreds or thousands of people.

That's incredibly valuable.

And what's even more interesting is that the value isn't necessarily in the hours worked. It's in what those hours leave behind.

Someone might spend 100 hours developing software that eventually saves a million people an hour each.

That's an extraordinary return on human effort.

Businesses are really systems for organizing human potential. They allow us to accomplish things that would be impossible individually.

But why should those capabilities be limited to businesses?

With AI, automation, open-source software, and shared infrastructure, we have an opportunity to give individuals access to the same organizational power.

Imagine having your own team of AI agents helping you manage your finances, schedule, health, projects, communications, and even your own businesses.

Not to make everyone work more, but to give everyone more freedom.

More time for family, creativity, community, and actually enjoying life.

That's increasingly what I want to build with 3DVR.

The organizational power of a business, accessible to everyone.

And ideally, open source, so we can build it together.
