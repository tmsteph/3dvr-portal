Community-Funded Projects

What if people could help useful projects get built—and clearly see what their support makes possible?

Concept only. This page does not offer investments, loans, interest, securities, or revenue shares. No payments are accepted here, and no returns are promised.

The idea

Inspired by community financing models such as Steward, we're exploring transparent support for open-source tools, local ventures, independent workers, and community infrastructure.

1. Discover an outcome

See who benefits, what will be delivered, and why resources are needed.

2. Follow the milestones

Understand budgets, progress, obstacles, and evidence of completion.

3. Build trust through openness

Learn what worked, what didn't, and which tools can be reused by others.

What we explore first

A static, mobile-friendly project showcase—no payments, no financial returns, and no real offers. We want to learn whether supporters can understand outcomes and track progress.

How it relates to UCN

Our Universal Credit Network explores transparent simulated IOU event records. It is not a functioning lending or payment product. The projects can share design principles without implying real-money capabilities.
