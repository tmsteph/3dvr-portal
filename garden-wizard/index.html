Garden Wizard
Jacket

Ancient spirit, modern workwear. A hand-sewn, martial-arts-inspired linen jacket for gardening, everyday life, and appropriate backstage work.

Design board

Illustrated design board image pending publication.

Natural materials

Black linen

Stagehand-inspired version.

Natural flax

Soft, breathable garden version.

Earth brown

Warm accents and optional contrast.

Features

Vertical overlapping front, soft polo collar, two wooden buttons, fitted sleeves, reinforced elbows, secure pockets and hip-length hem. Designed for hand sewing and repair.

Fit & process

Starting point: men's medium, 5′10½″, 160 lb. Actual body measurements are needed before cutting. Make a test garment first, then sew the linen version with reinforced backstitch, finished seams and hand-worked buttonholes.

From the garden to the stage

Keep the silhouette close-fitting and free of dangling ties around cables, lifts and rigging. Confirm fabric and garment suitability for each worksite; this concept is not certified protective clothing or a production-ready pattern.
