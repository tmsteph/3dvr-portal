Grow Your Own Linen

Start with a handful of flax seed. Learn the whole material cycle. Build simple tools. Share what works.

The experiment

First plot: about 3 × 3 ft

Plant fiber flax densely during San Diego's cool season. This first crop is about learning—not maximizing yield.

  • Use fiber flax (Linum usitatissimum), rather than ornamental flax.
  • Sow roughly ¼–½ inch deep and fairly close together for long, straight stems.
  • Keep the seedbed evenly moist while it establishes.
  • Pull mature plants rather than cutting them so the full fiber length is preserved.

Why 3DVR?

The crop is only half the project. Every processing step is an opportunity to document, simplify, and build open-source tools—from a flax break and hackle to small spinning and weaving systems.

Goal: a reproducible, human-scale path from soil to useful material.
