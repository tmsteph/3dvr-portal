3DVR Assembly · v0.1 direction

Organize people around what matters.

Assembly is the people-coordination layer for 3DVR: form a group, make roles clear, choose what matters now, record decisions, make commitments, share resources, and turn collective intention into visible outcomes.

Purpose→People→Initiative→Commitment→Outcome
Who?Members, circles, roles, relationships.
Why?Purpose, principles, shared direction.
Now?The initiatives that deserve attention.
Who owns it?Explicit commitments, owners, due dates.
What's blocked?Decisions, needs, dependencies, resources.
What happened?Outcomes and receipts instead of activity theater.
