Operating idea

Useful work builds trust.

  1. Start concreteFood, land, tools, time, skills, and simple needs create an easy first doorway.
  2. Help repeatedlySmall completed actions turn strangers into people who know they can rely on one another.
  3. Grow outwardHealthy local relationships can support care, learning, transport, projects, and shared infrastructure.

Why it belongs in the portal

Community is practical infrastructure.

The portal already tracks people, projects, notes, and community circles. This adds a real-world support graph: who has food, land, tools, skills, time, transport, or care to offer; who needs help; and what useful action can happen next.
