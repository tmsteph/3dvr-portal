# Living Water — Vortex Lab

An open-source 3DVR biomimicry experiment inspired by Viktor Schauberger's observations of water movement. This project tests hypotheses; it does not endorse unsupported free-energy claims.

## First prototype
Build a transparent recirculating vortex chamber with adjustable inlet angle, diameter (10–40 cm), and flow (1–15 L/min). Start with a low-voltage pump and enclosed reservoir. Include a straight-flow control chamber.

## Measurements
Record water volume, flow rate, inlet/outlet pressure, electrical pump power, temperature, sediment settling time, and mixing time. Run at least three replicates per configuration, controlling for volume, pump, and fluid temperature.

## Evaluation
Compare vortex and straight-flow baselines at matched flow and power. Calculate hydraulic power as pressure difference × volumetric flow; do not claim net energy generation from recirculation. Publish raw CSV measurements, photographs, and failures.

## Software roadmap
1. Browser-based educational vortex visualization (illustrative, not CFD).
2. Experiment configuration and data capture UI for mobile.
3. Plot performance against baseline and export open datasets.
4. Optional validated CFD and CAD models after physical measurements.

## Safety
Use low-voltage electrical components, GFCI-protected supplies, splash guards, pressure-rated tubing, and no sealed pressurized glass vessels. Never connect experiments to potable water systems without appropriate safeguards.

## Release criteria
Documented bill of materials, reproducible assembly guide, baseline measurements, and clear separation of observation, hypothesis, and verified result.
