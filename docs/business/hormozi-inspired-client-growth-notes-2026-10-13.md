## 3DVR offer
Sell customer outcomes, not websites, AI tools, editing hours, or automations. Candidate **Client Growth System**: conversion-focused website, lead capture, follow-ups, appointment booking, content workflow, and simple results dashboard. Scope and price each engagement against actual customer value and delivery cost.

## Operating model
1. Find a specific expensive customer problem.
2. Offer one measurable outcome and a simple scoped pilot.
3. Deliver manually with AI assistance; verify real-world results.
4. Standardize what works into repeatable processes and reusable open-source components.
5. Offer ongoing support only where it delivers continuing value.
6. Track revenue, gross margin, hours, retention, and customer outcomes—not vanity metrics.
