# Operator product direction

Operator is the everyday workspace for thinking, planning, and acting. The conversation is the primary interface; apps supply capabilities and records that Operator can summarize and visualize in the conversation. Apps themselves open as standalone pages.

## Arrive
Offer a quiet, useful starting point: resume conversations, open Today, Notes, Calendar, or Work, or choose a starter prompt. Preserve drafts when browsing tools. Do not fill the home screen with a large logo or invented summaries.

## Ask
Keep the composer available, make status readable, and keep the current response central. Use one message at a time with explicit previous/next controls and browser history. Preserve branches and account history.

## Review
Show the result and a clear next action. Open Helm and other tools directly with normal browser navigation. Do not embed their pages in Operator or intercept app links. Returning to the conversation must retain the draft and context.

## Visual direction
Warm charcoal surfaces, muted sage accents, restrained branding, readable line spacing, and mobile touch targets. Motion should communicate work, not compete with it.

## Next integration work
Pass app information through structured context and action results, and render useful summaries, cards, or visuals within the conversation. Keep full app interfaces on their own pages. Surface attention items and background work only from verified account-owned data; never invent activity or treat a static link as a live summary. Keep human approval for consequential actions.

## Verification
Check empty and populated chats at narrow mobile and desktop sizes; retain accessible history and branch controls; test browsing with a draft, opening a tool, and returning. Make the real streaming status visible. This pass changes presentation and entry points; it does not claim a new task feed or new record-editing API.
