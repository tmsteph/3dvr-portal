# 3DVR Revenue Operating System

## Objective

Build dependable income around verified capabilities, with automation reducing
research and follow-up rather than replacing judgment where a mistake could
damage trust.

## Three lanes

### 1. A/V employment and freelance coverage — cash now

- Buyer: production companies, staffing firms, hotels, corporate event teams,
  and touring organizations.
- Proof: the verified A/V resume and `thomas-av.3dvr.tech`.
- CTA: send the resume or discuss an available role/crew gap.
- Measure: replies, interviews, booked days, and effective hourly/day rate.

### 2. 3DVR launch subscription — recurring base

- Buyer: a founder or owner-led business with a real launch or website gap.
- Offer: personalized first-page draft, then $5/month keep-live, $20/month
  launch support, or $50/month active growth support.
- CTA: request a first draft.
- Measure: qualified founder conversations, drafts requested, paid starts,
  retained subscribers, and monthly recurring revenue.

### 3. Technical systems support — leverage

- Buyer: a small team losing time at the boundary of software, computers,
  networks, and workflows.
- Offer: $150 diagnostic, $500–$1,500 fixed sprint, or $50–$200/month support.
- CTA: describe the technical friction.
- Measure: diagnostics booked, sprint close rate, support MRR, and delivery
  hours per account.

## Automation rules

- Send only during Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–4:30 PM America/Los_Angeles.
- Direct email is allowed only when a usable route and a specific buyer problem
  are recorded.
- Forms, applications, social DMs, and page-only leads remain review queues.
- Every contact and touch must land in the canonical CRM with the exact body,
  campaign, variant, timestamp, delivery status, reply, and follow-up.
- Cap each experiment at 20–25 contacts before changing the offer or segment.
- Stop a lane after two consecutive experiments with no meaningful signal;
  improve the offer or targeting before adding volume.

## Weekly scorecard

Revenue is the primary metric. The weekly brief should report:

1. Cash collected and new recurring revenue.
2. Replies, interviews, booked calls, paid starts, and retained accounts.
3. Conversion rate by lane and message variant.
4. Leads blocked by missing route or missing enrichment.
5. The single highest-leverage change for the next week.

The system should optimize for one paid proof point at a time, then turn that
proof into a repeatable offer. More sends without a conversion signal are not
progress.
