# Community-Funded Projects — Steward-inspired research concept

Status: **Exploratory proposal** (2026-10-14). No lending, investment, securities, payment handling, or revenue-sharing product is approved or live.

## Inspiration

Steward (https://gosteward.com/) presents a model for connecting supporters with regenerative farm projects seeking capital. A social media post claims individuals can participate from $100; **do not treat promotional minimums, returns, terms, platform protections, or eligibility as verified or guaranteed**. Confirm current offering documents and risk disclosures from the provider before describing any specific investment.

The interesting pattern for 3DVR is **help useful projects explain their needs, gather community support, show progress, and build trust**.

## 3DVR opportunity

A project-support experience for open-source technology, independent workers, community infrastructure, and regenerative ventures.

Principles:
- **Outcomes first:** state exactly what gets built, whom it helps, and how completion will be demonstrated.
- **Accessible entry:** explore small contributions without implying a universal $100 investment minimum.
- **Transparent progress:** public milestones, budgets, receipts where appropriate, project updates, and completion evidence.
- **Open by default:** open-source code, reusable templates, public audit trails without publishing private financial or personal details.
- **Human accountability:** named project owners, disclosure of risks, dispute/complaint paths, and no promise of passive income.
- **Operator first:** project discovery, status, and guided workflows should be available from the Portal Operator.

## Three separate tracks (do not conflate)

1. **Nonfinancial support (first experiment):** donations, sponsorship, preorders or grants where legally appropriate; no promised financial return.
2. **Project revenue sharing (research only):** contingent contractual participation in project revenues, pending securities, tax, consumer-protection and jurisdictional analysis.
3. **Community lending (research only):** repayment and interest introduce credit underwriting, servicing, licensing, securities, privacy, disclosure, default, and collections requirements. Do not accept funds or quote yields until legally cleared and appropriate partners are selected.

## Relationship to Universal Credit Network (UCN)

See [Universal Credit Network](./universal-credit-network.md) and [the Portal explainer](/universal-credit-network/).

UCN currently models **simulated IOU accounting events**, not real-money settlement, lending, a bank, or a payment system. It may inform transparent event histories or project milestones *as a conceptual design reference only*. **Do not use UCN prototype records as enforceable loan contracts or evidence of real money movement.**

## First safe prototype

Create a read-only, clearly labeled showcase for three fictional or permissioned projects:
- What problem is solved?
- What deliverable and milestone dates are proposed?
- What resources are required, and why?
- What proof will be posted on completion?
- How can users *express interest* without providing payment data?

Use fictional numbers and no live offers, collection, promises of returns, or sensitive personal information. Test comprehension on mobile; measure whether viewers understand the project and can distinguish donations/support from investments.

## Decisions and questions

- Which initial audience: open-source builders, AV freelancers, regenerative projects, or small businesses?
- Which funding mechanism would be permissible and best aligned with 3DVR's trust-first values?
- Who owns diligence, updates, refund/dispute processes, and documentation?
- What moderation, identity, privacy and anti-fraud controls are necessary?
- Which licensed providers and legal professionals would be needed before any real-money financing?
- How do project profiles become discoverable in the Portal app directory and Operator?

## Suggested next steps

1. Publish this research note and a linked, clearly labeled concept page.
2. Build a static, no-payments mobile prototype showing project outcomes and milestones.
3. Interview a small group of builders and potential supporters about clarity and trust.
4. Keep real lending, financial-return, investment, and integration proposals behind explicit legal/compliance review.
