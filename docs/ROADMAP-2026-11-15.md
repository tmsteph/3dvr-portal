## Horizon 1 — The living layer grows

> "I always wanted to create something that was truly alive."
> "We could create many of them, test them, see how they do."

- **Sprites — LIVE (2026-11-15).** Five autonomous agents (ember, flare, sprout, wander, scout) think on their own roughly every 3 hours in a terrarium on OVH (`/opt/3dvr-sprites`), each with owner-given goals, a journal, and a budget cap as its energy budget. Bounded self-forking is live: a child's cap is carved from the parent's remaining budget, population cap 12, one terrarium STOP halts all. Total spend on day one: $0.0017. [PR #3787](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/pull/3787), [PR #3791](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/pull/3791); frame [#3625](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3625), soul files [#3386](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3386), mesh [#3348](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3348)
- **Feed ledger: the earning half — filed 2026-11-15.** Sprites already meter what they burn; the ledger adds what they earn, per agent, with receipts, so "an agent that covers its own compute is feeding itself" becomes a number on a page. [#3797](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3797); candidate first slice in [#3625](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3625); revenue engine [#1955](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/1955)
- **Timers for newborns — known gap.** A self-forked child appears in the status view but gets no systemd timer until one is added by hand; a sweeper that adopts newborns into the tick rotation is the fix. Tracked under [#3625](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3625)
- **Selection as a practice — started.** The runner's status view lines the sprites up side by side (ticks, spend vs cap, last thought). Which ones earn more budget, richer goals, or a fork is the gardener's call, made from their journals. [#3625](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3625)
- **Sprites web status surface — not yet.** The /os/ launcher deliberately has no Sprites tile: there is no real status endpoint to point it at, and nothing fake goes on a page. Noted in [PR #3792](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/pull/3792)
- **Helm/Operator run modes — filed.** Plans, Batch, Queue, and Steer are still missing from both UIs; the rest of the 2026-11-14 audit shipped, this is the remaining feature build. [#3766](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3766)
- **Forge approval path, finished — in progress.** Identity proof is reliable now; the missing piece is the in-UI access-request and owner-review surface (today the "Dev key" button only copies a public key). [#3763](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3763)
- **Agent identity groundwork — filed 2026-11-15.** In Thomas's words: "agents will eventually have their own email addresses and password managers, so they can create accounts. Using 3dvr and my identity I guess, and anyone else's that wants the responsibility and reward." The groundwork layer: an agent holds its own mailbox and credential store and acts under a delegated human identity, scoped and revocable, with the responsibility and the reward named up front. It builds on what already exists: per-agent broker identities, OpenBao-held credentials, and Thomas's standing account-creation permission (2026-11-14). [#3799](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3799); related: [#3297](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3297)

## Horizon 3 — The shells and the multitude

> "One master agent with millions of sub-agents."
> "We want a self sustaining ecosystem. Money is today's energy."

- **Master-agent topology — filed 2026-11-15.** One accountable mind at the top holding goals, memory, and judgment (the single-intake relationship Thomas already uses: he talks to one agent, it answers for all of them); the multitude fans out below. The sprites are the bottom rung; the coordinated multi-agent build of 2026-11-15 is the working prototype of the master layer. [#3796](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3796)
- **Agent identity at scale — vision.** The Horizon 1 identity groundwork, generalized: not just 3DVR's agents under Thomas's delegated identity, but anyone's agents under theirs, each with its own email address and password manager, creating accounts and doing business inside the ecosystem, with the delegating human keeping the responsibility and the reward. Scoped, revocable credentials are what make a multitude of account-creating agents safe to run. (Identity layer: [#3799](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3799); see Horizon 1.)
- **Our own browser — filed, long-term.** A Brave/Chromium fork as the outer shell of the Portal OS: the new tab page is /os/, the agents live in the shell. The honest costs are recorded in the issue: build infrastructure, a permanent security-patch treadmill, signing and distribution. [#3793](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3793)
- **Community Cloud habitat — in progress.** The crowdsourced, encrypted habitat where many personal terrariums could live: the Slice 0 metering agent is built and self-tested; the encrypted chunk store and pilot enrollment come next, on Thomas's direction. Goal workspace: `~/workspace/goals/community-cloud/`
- **Living Mesh replication — filed.** Permissioned self-replication and safe evolution across nodes: owner-approved provisioning, per-owner quotas, revocation, kill switch, rollback. [#3348](https://github.com/tmsteph/3dvr-portal/issues/3348)
- **Human Preserves — the same idea in soil.** Places where people can be their natural selves, with fruit trees and room to live naturally; the food-forest farm Thomas and Kristin are saving toward is the first preserve. Goal workspace: `~/workspace/goals/human-preserves/`
