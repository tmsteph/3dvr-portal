# Positive World Takeover

> Build systems so useful, humane, open, and empowering that people choose them.

3DVR does not aim to control the world. It aims to help people gain enough agency to shape their own part of it — then connect those efforts into a larger open commons.

## The loop

**State → Purpose → Opportunity → Build → Earn → Share → Community → Open Source → Repeat**

This extends the existing 3DVR movement model instead of replacing it.

1. **State** — Help a person get grounded enough to hear what matters.
2. **Purpose** — Surface interests, values, skills, frustrations, and callings.
3. **Opportunity** — Find real unmet needs that connect to those signals.
4. **Build** — Turn the smallest useful idea into a real project, service, tool, or experiment.
5. **Earn** — When appropriate, help the work become economically sustainable.
6. **Share** — Publish what was learned, not just what was sold.
7. **Community** — Connect people solving related problems so they can cooperate.
8. **Open Source** — Return reusable tools, knowledge, standards, and infrastructure to the commons.
9. **Repeat** — Let successful patterns become starting points for the next person or community.

## North star

**Increase human agency without creating dependence on 3DVR.**

A successful 3DVR system should leave people more capable of acting without us than they were before they arrived.

## Principles

### Positive-sum
Prefer systems where users, builders, communities, and the wider commons can all benefit.

### Voluntary
No coercion, manipulation, dark patterns, or artificial lock-in. People should be free to join, leave, export, fork, and rebuild.

### Local-first
Start with the person, household, neighborhood, team, or community closest to the need. Scale by connecting healthy local systems, not by flattening them.

### Open by default
Prefer open source, open protocols, portable data, documented interfaces, and reproducible processes whenever practical.

### Private by default
Personal reflection, identity, health, finances, relationships, and unfinished ideas should remain private unless a person deliberately shares them.

### Distributed ownership
Avoid making 3DVR the permanent gatekeeper. Encourage cooperatives, forks, self-hosting, local infrastructure, shared stewardship, and community ownership.

### Transparent AI
AI should explain what it is doing at the level needed for meaningful human oversight. Important decisions should remain reviewable and reversible.

### Measure flourishing, not addiction
Optimize for completed goals, stronger skills, sustainable income, useful relationships, healthier communities, and durable public goods — not time-on-platform.

## The AI operating loop

A 3DVR agent should continuously be able to:

1. Notice a need, bottleneck, demand signal, or opportunity.
2. Match it with a person's goals, skills, resources, and constraints.
3. Suggest the smallest useful experiment.
4. Help execute the experiment with permission.
5. Measure whether it actually helped.
6. Capture the reusable lesson.
7. Offer that lesson back to the community and open-source commons.
8. Improve the system from the result.

The agent can also identify purely economic opportunities, but profit is a means of sustaining useful work — not the final metric of human value.

## Fractal growth model

**Help one person → help them help ten people → give those ten tools to build → connect the builders → open-source what works → repeat.**

The same model should work at multiple scales:

- person
- family
- project
- business
- neighborhood
- community
- city
- network of communities

3DVR should act more like a kernel or protocol layer than an empire: shared primitives that let many independent systems coordinate.

## What this means for the Portal

The Portal should gradually become a self-steerable interface for this loop. Its job is not to expose every app equally. Its job is to understand where the person is, help determine the next useful action, and make that action easy to complete.

Core capabilities should eventually include:

- personal state and direction
- purpose and movement discovery
- demand/opportunity discovery
- project generation
- collaborators and community matching
- business/revenue tools
- agent-assisted execution
- learning and documentation
- publishing and open-source contribution
- feedback and outcome measurement

## Decision test

When evaluating a new 3DVR feature, project, partnership, or business model, ask:

> Does this increase people's capacity to create, cooperate, sustain themselves, and contribute back — while preserving their freedom?

If yes, it belongs in the movement.
