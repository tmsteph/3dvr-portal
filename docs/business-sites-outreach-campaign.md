# Business Sites Outreach Campaign

## Offer

3DVR makes a focused, mobile-ready first website for a small business, solo operator, or local service. The page is built around one real customer action: call, book, request a quote, or buy.

Front door: https://portal.3dvr.tech/business-sites/

Trust step: free first draft through https://portal.3dvr.tech/free-page/

Paid path: $5/month to keep a basic page live; $20–$50/month for ongoing launch, updates, and follow-up help.

## Target order

1. Service businesses with an obvious offer but a weak, outdated, or confusing website.
2. Solo operators who rely on referrals and have no single page to send.
3. New businesses with a Google Business Profile or social presence but no useful website.
4. Small agencies that need a reliable white-label landing-page partner.

Global is fine, but each contact must be relevant and publicly reachable. Do not buy or scrape huge lists before a small test proves the message.

## 25-contact test

- 10 service businesses with a visible customer-flow problem
- 5 solo operators with a strong skill and no clear web home
- 5 new businesses with a public launch signal
- 5 small agencies that may need overflow help

Send at most 5 initial commercial emails per day until deliverability and reply quality are verified. One follow-up maximum, at least 21 days later, only to people who did not opt out. Retire anyone who says no, stop, unsubscribe, or do not contact.

Thomas

## Stop/scale rule

After 25 targeted touches:

- 3+ specific replies: continue the same segment and message.
- 1–2 specific replies: revise the promise or target before sending more.
- 0 specific replies: stop outbound and rewrite the offer.
- Any meaningful complaint or deliverability warning: stop immediately and investigate.

The purpose of the first batch is learning and a first customer, not volume theater.

## Compliance gates

Every commercial email needs a truthful sender, clear commercial disclosure, valid postal address, and working opt-out. Do not use purchased lists, fake personalization, invented audits, or guaranteed-results language. Follow local law where the recipient is located.
