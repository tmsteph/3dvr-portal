Open hardware / interactive concept v0.1

TriGrip

One phone. Infinite configurations. A collapsible triangular grip, universal mounting interface, and detachable keyboard + controls.

✣◉✥

Handheld

Keyboard and game controls dock below the phone for mobile use.

Universal attachment system

Swap the base without replacing the phone cradle. Every mount needs its own rated retention mechanism.

Input modules

Interaction is a design simulation; no physical device or Bluetooth pairing is controlled by this page.

Build sequence

  1. Print hinge + triangular frame, test collapsed pocket thickness and pinch points.
  2. Test locking angles and mechanical retention with a dummy phone mass.
  3. Prototype clip, clamp, strap and tripod adapter independently.
  4. Build detachable keyboard/controller rail, USB-C HID and Bluetooth HID variants.
  5. Publish CAD, BOM, assembly guides and load-test results.

Not yet load-rated. Do not use suction, adhesive, magnets, or wearable mounts over people or hazards until tested. Vehicle use must not obstruct driving.
