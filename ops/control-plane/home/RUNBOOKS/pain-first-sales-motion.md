# Pain-First Sales Motion

Date: 2026-09-12
Status: active default for fresh cold outreach

## Rule

For fresh cold leads, prefer a verified problem over a generic capability pitch.

Sequence:

`visible pain -> public evidence -> small paid diagnostic -> deliver -> expand only if justified`

Canonical playbook: `docs/pain-first-diagnostic-outreach.md`
Operator desk: `/sales/pain-first.html`

## Default first offer

- $99 diagnostic
- acceptable range: $50-$200 when scope clearly supports it
- deliver a useful findings summary and fix plan
- keep remediation as a separate scope unless explicitly included

## Lead gate

Before outreach, record:

- primary pain
- public evidence / source
- severity and urgency
- diagnostic price
- next best action

Skip the lead if the pain cannot be verified without inventing details.

## Messaging

The first note should contain:

1. one observable problem,
2. one practical consequence,
3. one small paid diagnostic,
4. one easy yes/no ask.

Do not lead with a list of 3DVR capabilities.

## Boundaries

- Fresh cold leads may use the autonomous outreach lane.
- Known personal or professional contacts remain approval-gated.
- Send only during normal recipient business hours.
- Existing commercial disclosure, postal address, opt-out, and deliverability gates remain mandatory.
- Security-sensitive findings must be described minimally and non-alarmingly; do not disclose exploit details or perform invasive verification without authorization.

## First test

Run 10 qualified fresh leads:

- 5 owner-led service businesses
- 3 professional-services firms
- 2 agencies or technical operators

Continue after 10 only if there are at least 2 real replies or 1 paid diagnostic. Zero meaningful replies means stop and improve the problem/offer rather than increasing volume.
