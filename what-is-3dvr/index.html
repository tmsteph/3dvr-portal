What is 3DVR?

Redesign the world.
Open source.

3DVR is a worldwide project to empower individuals. We want people everywhere to have more power to understand, own, build, repair, share, and improve the systems they depend on.

3DVR is bigger than a website, a company, or a piece of software. It is an open-source movement toward a world that ordinary people can understand and shape for themselves.

01

3DVR is the bigger project.

The long-term mission is to redesign everyday life around openness, individual agency, shared knowledge, local resilience, and tools people can actually understand and control.

02

3DVR.tech is our technology company.

3DVR.tech builds software, AI, computing systems, infrastructure, and open-source technology that help move the wider 3DVR mission forward.

03

Portal.3DVR.tech is our main project right now.

Portal is becoming a simple interface between people and computing: projects, communication, work, communities, AI agents, and eventually a user-owned digital twin that can help operate the software around you.

The world we want

Technology should increase human freedom, not dependency.

Today, much of life depends on systems that are difficult to inspect, repair, change, or leave. 3DVR asks what happens when we build the opposite: systems whose knowledge is shared, whose parts can be replaced, whose rules can be understood, and whose benefits can reach people anywhere in the world.

That can mean software and computers, but it can also mean education, housing, food, manufacturing, businesses, local infrastructure, community organization, and the everyday tools people use to live.
