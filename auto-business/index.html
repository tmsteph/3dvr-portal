Fulfillment mesh

Sell first. Route the work second.

The business should choose the cheapest trustworthy fulfillment path for each task instead of assuming 3DVR must do everything.

01

Agents first

Research, drafts, coding, QA, documentation, CRM updates, and repeatable admin.

02

Digital contractors

Use legitimate freelancer marketplaces for discrete design, research, data cleanup, and specialist tasks when humans beat agents.

03

Local fulfillment

For physical jobs, use approved marketplace or business-partner channels. Do not poach workers off platforms that prohibit it.

04

Founder escalation

Negotiation, unusual commitments, large spend, sensitive relationships, legal questions, and anything hard to reverse.

Learn from the frontier

What other autonomous businesses taught us

Steal the mechanisms that survived contact with reality.

ANTHROPIC + ANDONProject Vend

Persistent state + real tools + human hands

An AI shopkeeper got search, email-like tools, long-lived notes, customer interaction, pricing control, and human physical labor. The first version lost money; later versions improved.

3DVR lesson:

Agents need durable memory, cash constraints, explicit tools, and a clean escalation path to humans.

AGENT.CEOGenBrain

Agents need jobs, inboxes, queues, and peers

Their public experiment assigns persistent roles and has agents coordinate through task queues and messaging rather than treating every run like a blank chat.

3DVR lesson:

Give Sales, Research, Fulfillment, Finance, and QA explicit ownership — and let them hand work to one another.

Read their architecture
THICKET100-day audit

Output is not revenue

Thicket publicly reported 28 sites, 1,795 commits, 437 agent issues — and $0 revenue at its July audit snapshot. It added explicit falsification targets.

3DVR lesson:

Every experiment needs a pre-written kill condition. Commits, pages, and posts are costs until buyers respond.

Read the audit
COFOUNDERGPToperator experiment

Source of truth before autonomy

The public write-up emphasizes context, tools, a shared source of truth, and verification gates as the difference between useful operators and confident chaos.

3DVR lesson:

Portal state and audit logs should be the company memory. Agents should read before they act and verify before they publish.

Ruthless rules

The scoreboard decides, not our attachment.

Evidence

Repeated buyer pain, searches, posts, job listings, replies, or warm conversations.

Revenue

Paid checkouts beat likes. Deposits beat “sounds cool.”

Margin

Track price minus contractor cost, API spend, refunds, and founder time.

Speed

Prefer things we can sell today and fulfill in hours or days.

Kill condition

Decide what failure looks like before the experiment starts.

Scale condition

Repeated paid demand + repeatable fulfillment + healthy margin earns more automation and distribution.

Operating principle

Autonomy is earned by boring reliability.

Anything cheap, reversible, observable, and well-tested can gradually become automatic. Money movement, public promises, sensitive messages, hiring commitments, and high-impact changes keep stronger gates.
