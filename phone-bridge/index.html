01 / FOUNDATION

Secure pairing and device status

QR pairing, explicit permissions, authenticated encrypted sessions, revocation, and clear connection status.

02 / LOCAL FIRST

SMS + Bluetooth calling

Use Android-approved messaging capabilities and Linux Bluetooth HFP. Verify OS and carrier restrictions before claiming support.

03 / REMOTE

Browser-to-phone bridge

Encrypted signaling, remote call controls, and a carefully tested audio path. Ordinary cellular audio capture may be restricted by Android.

04 / OPERATOR

Human-approved AI assistance

Call preparation, consent-aware summaries, explicit approval for outbound calls and commitments, and optional third-party voice providers.
