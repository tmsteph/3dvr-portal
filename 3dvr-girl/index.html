The Woods Chapter

The portal had always opened into light. This time, it opened into trees.

3DVR Girl followed the signal past the last mapped node, where the network thinned into moss, cedar, and old paths with no coordinates. That was where she met Kayla — not another avatar or a copy, but a keeper of a different layer of the system.

Kayla called it the Root Network: the part of reality that was connected long before anyone invented cables. She taught 3DVR Girl to listen for patterns in wind, water, and memory. In return, 3DVR Girl showed Kayla how to carry those patterns through the portal without flattening them into data.

By sunset, they understood the same thing from opposite directions: technology and nature were never enemies. They were two interfaces to the same living system.

And somewhere deeper in the woods, another portal woke up.
