3DVR DAO + Credits

What if useful work for the network created verifiable value? We are exploring a community economy inspired by GUN, SEA, AXE, and Proof of Propagation.

Research stage · no token sale

3. VerificationPeers check provenance, history, and rules instead of trusting one central operator.

5. CoordinationCredits and reputation can inform proposals, rewards, grants, and governance.

Where the idea comes from

GUN creator Mark Nadal described AXE as the Advanced eXchange Equation. Its published roadmap goes beyond peer routing: Stage 3 proposed contracts for bandwidth and relay service, with bandwidth tokenized by metering throughput. The same AXE documentation describes SEA as a necessary cryptographic primitive and references working identity and wallet demonstrations.

The accompanying “Trusting Strangers with Axes” cartoon explains the economic idea with receipts and child coins: useful network activity leaves evidence, that evidence can be combined into new value, and coin history can help peers reason about cheating and Sybil behavior.

Our extension

AXE focused heavily on bandwidth. 3DVR can test the same principle across the broader open-source organism.

  • Relay and bandwidth
  • Storage and compute
  • Code contributions
  • Documentation and testing
  • Useful AI-agent work
  • Community support and project stewardship

What it is not yet

This page does not launch a cryptocurrency, promise financial value, or create an investment fund. A real transferable token, pooled treasury, or AI-managed investment fund would require substantially stronger settlement rules plus legal and regulatory review.

The first experiment should be contribution credits, not speculation.

First prototype

  1. Create a SEA identity for each participating node or contributor.
  2. Record signed receipts for a small class of objectively useful work.
  3. Verify receipts from independent peers and preserve their provenance in GUN.
  4. Issue non-cash test credits with no promised market value.
  5. Use those credits alongside reputation for small community proposals and grants.
  6. Only after adversarial testing decide whether transferability or an external settlement layer is useful.

Why not just put everything on a blockchain?

Because most of the 3DVR organism is communication, identity, collaboration, and shared state. GUN is designed for highly available peer-to-peer data, while a blockchain-style consensus layer is most valuable where irreversible financial settlement or double-spend protection actually requires it. The goal is to use each tool where it is strongest rather than making every interaction a transaction.
