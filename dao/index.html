A possible DAO for 3DVR

Help build 3DVR.
Help decide what it becomes.

3DVR is open source. As more people contribute, we want responsibility to spread too — not just the code.

In plain English

A 3DVR DAO would be a way for contributors to help decide what gets built, what gets funded, and how shared resources are used. People earn trust by doing useful work. Important decisions stay public.

Early experiment · not a token sale or investment product

How it could work

The idea starts with contribution and governance. Cryptocurrency is optional, not the point.

1. ContributeWrite code, test, document, host infrastructure, help users, or do other useful work.
2. Prove the workA signed receipt records what happened and who participated.
3. Build trustUseful contributions build reputation and experimental 3DVR Credits.
4. Help decideContributors can propose ideas, vote, delegate, and help guide shared resources.

What would the community actually govern?

Not everything needs a vote. The goal is to share meaningful decisions where community participation makes 3DVR stronger.

What we build

Roadmap priorities, public proposals, standards, and long-term project direction.

What we fund

Contributor bounties, grants, hardware, servers, research, and shared infrastructure.

Who takes responsibility

Stewards, delegated roles, maintainers, and gradually decentralized decision rights.

How shared capital is used

Potentially, a separately governed pool for aligned businesses, public goods, infrastructure, or investments.

Could 3DVR eventually invest?

Potentially, through a separate community-governed capital pool. Its mandate could support 3DVR businesses, open-source public goods, hardware, infrastructure, startups, crypto, or other assets that strengthen the ecosystem.

This prototype does not pool money, sell a token, or promise returns. A real investment vehicle would need the appropriate legal structure, custody, tax, and regulatory review before launch.

Why GUN + AXE?

GUN's older AXE research explored a similar idea: useful network work creates signed receipts, and those receipts can become part of a system for value and trust.
