Research idea

3DVR DAO

People and computers do useful work. The network records it. Useful work can earn credits. The community can use those credits to help decide what 3DVR builds, funds, and rewards.

Experiment only · no token sale
1. Do useful workHost, relay, compute, code, test, document, or help someone.
2. Get a receiptA signed record shows what happened and who took part.
3. Earn creditsVerified work can create 3DVR Credits.
4. Help governCredits and reputation can help guide projects, treasury spending, and rewards.

How 3DVR could be governed

The DAO would give contributors a real voice without turning 3DVR into simple one-token-one-vote rule.

  • Anyone can make a public proposal.
  • Useful work builds credits and reputation.
  • Contributors can vote directly or delegate their voice.
  • Treasury spending stays public and can require multiple approvals.
  • Maintainers keep limited safety and security powers early on, then decentralize them as the system matures.

What could the treasury fund?

3DVR revenue, donations, grants, or future protocol fees could feed a transparent community treasury.

  • Open-source 3DVR projects and contributor bounties
  • Servers, hardware, research, and shared infrastructure
  • Small grants to community builders
  • New businesses or tools that fit the 3DVR mission

Could there be a 3DVR investment fund?

Potentially. A separately governed pool could eventually invest part of the treasury in projects, startups, crypto, or other assets the community believes strengthen the ecosystem.

AI could research opportunities, compare risk, watch the portfolio, and suggest allocations. The community would approve the rules and major moves instead of giving an AI unlimited control of real money.

If real money is pooled or people expect investment returns, we would need a proper legal structure and regulatory review before launching it.

Inspired by GUN + AXE

GUN's AXE research explored rewarding useful network service instead of mining coins by wasting energy. Its “Trusting Strangers with Axes” example uses signed receipts and coin history to make useful work verifiable.

3DVR could extend that idea beyond bandwidth to storage, compute, code, testing, documentation, AI-agent work, and community help.

First step

Build simple test credits with GUN + SEA. No real money. No speculation. Use them first for contribution tracking and small governance experiments. If that works, we can decide later whether a transferable crypto token, community treasury, or investment layer makes sense.
