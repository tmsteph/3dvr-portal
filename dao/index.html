A possible DAO for 3DVR

Help build 3DVR.
Help decide what it becomes.

3DVR is open source. As more people help build it, we want more people to share responsibility for where it goes next.

What is a DAO?

DAO means Decentralized Autonomous Organization. In simple terms, it is a group that uses shared rules, verifiable records, and collective decision-making instead of leaving everything to one person.

A DAO does not need its own coin. Tokens are one possible tool for membership, voting, fundraising, or transferable value. Reputation, earned credits, membership lists, multisig wallets, and other governance systems can also be used.

Blockchain is common because it can make rules and money independently verifiable. It is not the same thing as a coin, and it does not have to be the first thing we build.

Can a DAO exist without a blockchain? Read the deeper GUN + SEA + AXE explanation →

“Autonomous” does not mean AI runs the organization. It means software can help carry out rules the group already agreed on.

What would that mean for 3DVR?

Start with contribution records, earned trust, proposals, and voting. People who actually help 3DVR could earn more responsibility and a stronger voice over time. A future coin or token remains optional; the DAO should work without one.

1. HelpBuild, test, write, host, teach, organize, or support the community.
2. Record itKeep a clear record of useful work and who took part.
3. Build trustConsistent useful work can earn recognition, credits, and more responsibility.
4. Help decideMake proposals, vote, or trust someone else to vote for you.

Quick next experiment

Start small with 3DVR Credits: record one useful contribution in GUN, sign the receipt with SEA, verify who approved it, then issue a test credit that keeps a link back to the work that earned it.

GUNDistribute contribution records across peers instead of depending on one central database.
SEASign receipts so we can verify who approved an event.
CreditCreate earned, non-cash reputation or points with provenance.
Then AXEExplore rewarding useful network service and carrying those receipts across the network.

GUN and cryptographic signatures can reduce how much we need to trust one server or administrator, but they do not automatically make every value immutable or solve financial double-spending. If the experiment eventually manages real pooled money, a separate smart-contract or multisig treasury could provide stronger financial guarantees.

Where could a coin fit?

A coin is a separate experiment, not a requirement for the DAO. We should add one only when it solves a real problem better than credits or reputation.

  • Transferable value between people outside 3DVR
  • Permissionless fundraising or ownership
  • On-chain incentives for network services
  • A community decision that a market-based token is useful

What could the community decide?

  • Which 3DVR projects should be priorities
  • Which contributors should receive grants or rewards
  • What servers, hardware, and research should be funded
  • Who should take responsibility for important parts of the project
  • How a future community fund should be used

Could 3DVR invest money too?

Possibly, but it should start as a separate experiment rather than pretending to represent all of 3DVR. One portal app could test an AI-assisted community fund with clearly defined membership, proposals, voting, and treasury rules.

AI could help research ideas and explain risks, but people would set the rules and approve important decisions. A real investment fund would need the right legal and financial structure before handling pooled money.

Where did the idea come from?

Older GUN and AXE research explored a related idea: reward useful work on a network and keep a clear history of where that value came from. We are borrowing the spirit of that idea. A future 3DVR coin or token remains an option, not a prerequisite.
