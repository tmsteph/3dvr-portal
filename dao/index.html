What the DAO can govern

  • Roadmap priorities and public proposals
  • Contributor grants, bounties, and rewards
  • Shared servers, hardware, research, and infrastructure
  • Standards, stewards, and delegated responsibilities
  • Eventually: a separately governed community investment mandate

Maintainers can keep narrow safety and security powers early, with those powers explicitly reduced as governance proves itself.

Could 3DVR invest?

Potentially, through a separately governed capital pool. Its mandate could include 3DVR businesses, open-source public goods, hardware, infrastructure, startups, crypto, or other assets that strengthen the ecosystem. AI could continuously research and explain opportunities, while community rules, human approvals, and transparent receipts control execution.

This prototype does not pool money, sell a token, or promise returns. A real investment vehicle would need the appropriate legal structure, custody, tax, and regulatory review before launch.
