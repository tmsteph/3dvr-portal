A possible DAO for 3DVR

Help build 3DVR.
Help decide what it becomes.

3DVR is open source. As more people help build it, we want more people to share responsibility for where it goes next.

What is a DAO?

DAO means Decentralized Autonomous Organization. In simple terms, it is a group that uses shared rules, public records, and voting to make decisions together instead of leaving everything to one person.

“Autonomous” does not mean AI runs the organization. It means software can help carry out rules the group already agreed on.

What would that mean for 3DVR?

People who actually help 3DVR could earn more trust and a stronger voice over time. A 3DVR coin or token could eventually be part of that system, and selling one could be one way to fund the ecosystem. The DAO is bigger than the coin: the goal is to share responsibility, ownership, and value.

1. HelpBuild, test, write, host, teach, organize, or support the community.
2. Record itKeep a clear record of useful work and who took part.
3. Build trustConsistent useful work can earn recognition, credits, and more responsibility.
4. Help decideMake proposals, vote, or trust someone else to vote for you.

What could the community decide?

  • Which 3DVR projects should be priorities
  • Which contributors should receive grants or rewards
  • What servers, hardware, and research should be funded
  • Who should take responsibility for important parts of the project
  • How a future community fund should be used

Could 3DVR invest money too?

Possibly. A separate community fund could someday support 3DVR businesses, open-source projects, hardware, infrastructure, startups, or other investments that fit the mission.

AI could help research ideas and explain risks, but people would set the rules and approve important decisions. A real investment fund would need the right legal and financial structure before handling pooled money.

Where did the idea come from?

Older GUN and AXE research explored a related idea: reward useful work on a network and keep a clear history of where that value came from. We are borrowing the spirit of that idea. A future 3DVR coin or token remains an option if it becomes useful to the community.
