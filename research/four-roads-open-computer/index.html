Four Roads to an Open Computer

Sipeed, StarFive, BeagleBoard, and LattePanda each solve a different layer of the problem. Together they suggest a practical path from today's development boards toward computers communities can understand, repair, modify, and eventually manufacture.

Core thesis

A practical open computer can be built layer by layer: deeper silicon, rapid productization, genuine community openness, and replaceable compute modules.

3DVR direction

Learn on real RISC-V hardware, define a module-neutral carrier contract, contribute upstream, and move toward FPGA and SoC work only after the lower layers are understood.
