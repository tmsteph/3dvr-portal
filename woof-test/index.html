Woof test

An experiment. Underdog's Woof is a tiny text-to-speech model that runs entirely in this browser tab: Python and numpy compiled to WebAssembly, model weights loaded once (about 41 MB), then every word is generated on your device. Nothing you type is sent to a server. It is known for reading the hard stuff out loud correctly: phone numbers, dates, prices and codes.

Model and code: huggingface.co/ConwayResearch/Underdog-Woof. Runtime: Pyodide (CPython + numpy in WebAssembly). First load is roughly 55 MB all-in; after that the model is cached and generation happens on-device, offline included. Generation speed depends on your device: expect a few seconds per sentence.
