Default cold-sales motion

Find the pain. Sell the diagnostic.

Stop asking strangers to imagine why they need 3DVR. Find one public, verifiable problem and offer one small paid next step.

Visible pain → precise note → paid diagnostic → excellent delivery → larger fix

1 · Find

Visible pain

Use a public signal: broken customer path, dead link, confusing mobile flow, intake friction, or another real operational problem.

2 · Verify

Evidence first

Record what you actually saw and where. Never invent an audit finding just to personalize a message.

3 · Offer

$99 diagnostic

Default to a useful findings + fix-plan deliverable. Use roughly $50–$200 when scope clearly calls for it.

4 · Expand

Earn the next job

Only propose remediation, automation, maintenance, Builder, or Embedded work when the diagnostic supports it.

Message pattern

One problem. One consequence. One ask.

Hi [Business] team, I noticed [specific public problem]. That can make it harder for customers to [practical consequence]. I can check the path end to end and send you a short findings + fix plan for $99. No rebuild required. Want me to take a look? Thomas

The existing outreach sender still adds the required commercial disclosure, postal address, and opt-out footer.

Qualification gate

Skip weak leads.

  • ✓ Public signal is real and easy to verify.
  • ✓ Problem plausibly costs time, leads, reliability, or trust.
  • ✓ Diagnostic can be useful without privileged access.
  • ✓ First paid step is narrow and reversible.
  • ✓ Message can stay factual and non-alarming.

First experiment

10 qualified fresh leads

Five owner-led service businesses, three professional-services firms, and two agencies or technical operators. No known contacts in the cold-outreach lane.

Stop / scale rule

After 10 qualified touches: continue if we get at least two real replies or one paid diagnostic. If we get zero meaningful replies, stop and improve the problem quality or offer instead of increasing volume. Pause immediately on a factual error, complaint, or deliverability warning.
