Core lore

The Portal is not one place.

At first, the Portal looked like software: a collection of pages, tools, worlds, and experiments. Then the crew began finding the same patterns outside the screen.

Kayla calls that deeper layer the Root Network. 3DVR Girl calls it the signal. Thomas calls it something that should probably be open source. 3DVR Bot keeps taking notes.

The crew's long arc is simple: build technology that reconnects people with their own creativity, communities, tools, and physical world instead of pulling them away from it.

Where the universe can go next

01

The Woods Chapter

3DVR Girl follows an unmapped portal into the forest and meets Kayla. The Root Network becomes the first proof that the Portal reaches far beyond computers.

02

Signal Across Borders

Thomas, David, and Brandon build together across distance. A creative pipeline between California and Guatemala slowly becomes a distributed studio for open worlds.

03

The Tide Signal

Mark starts documenting repeating patterns in waves, photographs, timing, physics, and code. The images look ordinary until 3DVR Bot overlays the data.

04

Bot Learns the Crew

3DVR Bot was supposed to organize the work. Instead, it starts learning everyone's habits, jokes, unfinished ideas, and ways of solving problems—and develops a personality of its own.

05

Open Source Civilization

The experiments stop being separate projects. Software, hardware, art, food, clothes, learning, community, and local production begin fitting together into one open system for everyday life.
