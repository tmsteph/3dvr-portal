Core lore

Build the future, then go live in it.

The Portal begins as software: websites, tools, virtual spaces, little experiments, and machines connected by people in different places.

But the crew keeps pushing it outward—into studios, beaches, forests, workshops, events, homes, communities, and whatever physical thing they decide to build next.

The long arc is an open-source world where technology gives people more control over their lives instead of demanding more of their attention. The fictional universe lets us exaggerate that journey while the real crew keeps building it.

Story threads · provisional canon

Where the universe can go next

01

The Woods Chapter

3DVR Girl and Kayla take a Portal job far from the studio. They work well together immediately, but neither one seems interested in explaining exactly how they met.

02

The Distributed Studio

Thomas, David, and Brandon figure out how to build one project while working between California and Guatemala. What starts as remote collaboration gradually becomes a strange little international studio.

03

Mark's Field Lab

Mark keeps one foot in serious software engineering and the other outside. Cameras, surf sessions, electronics, code, and physics experiments become a practical field lab for ideas the rest of the crew only understands after he demonstrates them.

04

Bot Joins the Crew

3DVR Bot is introduced as a utility machine. Within a week it has favorite people, strong opinions about cable management, a collection of mysterious beeps, and a habit of saving the day while pretending nothing unusual happened.

05

Esai Makes It Real

The crew can build almost anything, but prototypes do not pay for themselves. Esai starts turning chaotic ideas into prices, plans, customers, budgets, and operations—without letting the builders completely lose their minds.

06

Open Source Civilization

The projects stop feeling separate. Software, hardware, art, clothes, learning, community, local production, and everyday tools begin fitting together into a world the crew can actually inhabit.
