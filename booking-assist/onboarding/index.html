Booking Assist setup

Connect once. We handle the repetitive bridge.

The production onboarding flow is being hardened for multiple customers. For the founding beta, setup uses the existing 3DVR secure access system and a short assisted connection session.

01

Create your secure workspace

Your account gets an isolated Booking Assist identity, browser workspace, connector aliases, policies, and audit history. Your credentials are never committed to GitHub or stored in the public site.

02

Connect Lighthouse, IATSE, and Encore/UKG

Preferred path: sign in directly inside the isolated browser session and preserve the authenticated session. When a stored credential is necessary, it goes through the 3DVR Secrets Broker into encrypted secret storage and is exposed only to the connector that needs it.

Open secure access
03

Choose your rules

Tell Booking Assist what should count as booked, unavailable, or protected, plus any rules that should always require your approval. The first preset is simple: Lighthouse booked dates update IATSE availability and trigger the corresponding Encore time-off workflow.

04

Let the agent reconcile

The backend checks connection health, reads schedule state, detects mismatches, queues required updates, and records evidence of each action. MFA, CAPTCHA, unusual conflicts, and destructive or high-impact actions stop for you.

Security rule: do not paste Lighthouse, IATSE, Encore, Microsoft, or UKG passwords into chat. The finished onboarding flow will provide connector-specific secure inputs or direct sign-in sessions on the 3DVR control plane.
