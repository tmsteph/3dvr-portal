From Browser Life to Digital Organism

This experiment revives the 2021 Browser Life idea—creatures as data-producing organisms—and gives it a home beside 3DVR's Digital Organism. One studies emergent artificial life; the other studies durable personal intelligence. Both ask the same question: what happens when a system can keep history and adapt?
