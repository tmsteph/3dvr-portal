What are we exploring?

Could a simple beginning give rise to the complexity of a universe? This first experiment lets an expanding visual pulse reveal a Mandelbulb point cloud. Change its power, orbit it, and explore the detail.

The fractal is defined in advance. The pulse reveals it; it does not create matter, simulate gravity, or explain the Big Bang. Space, time, and distances here are arbitrary visualization units. Similar-looking patterns alone cannot establish that a model describes our universe.

Next: let structures emerge from documented evolving rules, then test the results against observations.
