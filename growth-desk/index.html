Executable workflow

Lead → sale → learning.

The runtime stores each opportunity as one shared work item and advances it only when the next event has evidence.

1Research

Find a real opportunity and capture source evidence.

2Qualify

Confirm fit, route, need, and the next best action.

3Outreach

Prepare and send a bounded message under policy.

4Listen

Capture delivery, replies, failures, and external waits.

5Follow up

Schedule the next touch so the opportunity cannot disappear.

6Proposal

Turn interest into concrete scope, price, and next step.

7Payment

Verify the paid result instead of inferring success.

8Learn

Update CRM, message/offer lessons, and the next experiment.
