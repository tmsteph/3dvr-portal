The Stack

Civilization is a set of recipes.

01 Everyday life

Food, clothing, shelter, tools, water, and the objects around us.

 02 Machines

Repairable hardware, fabrication, materials, energy, and supply chains.

 03 Computing

Linux, RISC-V, open AI, personal servers, and understandable computers.

 04 Business

Open tools for finding work, serving people, trading value, and staying independent.

 05 Knowledge

Document what works so the next builder starts farther ahead.

 06 Community

People sharing skills, land, labor, designs, tools, teaching, and support.

Start Small

Civilizations begin with a workshop.

Pick one useful thing. Build it. Publish the whole recipe. Let somebody else reproduce it. Improve the design from what both of you learn.

  1. 1
    Pick one project.

    Something useful enough to finish and test now.

  2. 2
    Publish the recipe.

    Designs, materials, cost, instructions, repair, and lessons.

  3. 3
    Hand it forward.

    Make the next person's starting point better than yours.
