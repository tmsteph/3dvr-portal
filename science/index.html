Protocol preview

Fill in the experiment and build a protocol. The goal is not to sound scientific; the goal is to make the test repeatable.

Replication is the superpower. Publish the method, observations, raw measurements, uncertainty, and failures. Invite someone else to try it. A result becomes more useful when other people can challenge it.

Focus areas

Where the lab speeds up 3DVR delivery

🧬

Evidence loops

Draft hypotheses, publish quick tests, and keep observations synced across devices.

  • Store methods, controls, and outcomes inside shared Gun nodes.
  • Tag sessions with teammates so reviews and replications are tracked automatically.
  • Link results to VR prototypes to validate comfort and flow.
📡

Sensing & telemetry

Mirror wearables, breathwork, or controller data inside collaborative dashboards.

  • Stream time-series checkpoints into gun.get('science').get('signals').
  • Compare pre/post metrics for movement, focus, and calm.
  • Map thresholds to rewards so healthy habits unlock in-portal boosts.
🛰️

Simulation to production

Translate findings into VR-ready interactions with safety and ergonomics baked in.

  • Prototype interaction patterns, then export shared playbooks for builders.
  • Attach safety and comfort checks to every experimental branch.
  • Publish reference data so teams can remix proven flows quickly.

Workflow

From idea to VR deployment

Follow a repeatable cycle that keeps science paired with production builds.

1 · Plan

Log hypotheses and protocols in notes, then route approvals through tasks.

2 · Run

Collect sensor snapshots in gun.get('science').get('runs') for cross-session review.

3 · Sync

Compare cohorts, annotate anomalies, and mark replications as they land from relays.

4 · Ship

Export validated playbooks and share them with builders shipping the next VR features.
