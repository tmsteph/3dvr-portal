The Open
Wizard Robe

From flax to fabric. From plants to color. From intention to ritual. A practical, beautiful robe that anyone can make, adapt, repair, and share.

Project status: concept / pattern development

No tested sewing pattern has been published yet. This page is the open design brief and an invitation to collaborate.

The garment

A generous hooded wrap robe made from breathable 100% linen, with flowing sleeves, deep pockets, a replaceable belt, reinforced seams, and optional embroidered symbols. Designed for real daily use, not disposable costume wear.

Colors from the earth

Onion skins · gold
Indigo · midnight
Madder · rust
Walnut hull · brown

Results vary with linen preparation, dye concentration, water chemistry and mordants. Document each recipe, test samples, and check wash and light fastness. Cellulose fibers like linen often benefit from tannin and an appropriate alum process; indigo requires its own vat process.

Herbs, oils & blessing

Wash and dry the finished robe, then choose a personal blessing: a spoken intention, quiet meditation, prayer, or a moment of gratitude. Lavender and rosemary can be kept in a removable sachet. Avoid applying undiluted essential oils to skin or clothing; oils may irritate skin or stain fabric, and smoke or flame should be kept away from the garment.

May this garment remind its wearer to act with wisdom, create with courage, and care for the world around them.

Open craft roadmap

  1. Draft size-inclusive printable SVG and PDF patterns.
  2. Sew and wear-test a linen prototype.
  3. Publish fabric usage, construction steps, and repair guide.
  4. Test and document reproducible natural dye recipes.
  5. Share optional herb sachets, embroidery, and blessing rituals.
