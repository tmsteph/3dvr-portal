The Open
Wizard Robe

A linen robe grown from the earth, colored by plants, blessed with herbs and intention, and shared freely with the world.

Grow the plants. Weave the cloth. Dye the fibers. Bless the garment. Share the knowledge.

The garment

A practical, flowing, hooded wrap robe made from breathable 100% flax linen. Deep pockets, replaceable tie belt, roomy sleeves, and modular hood. Designed for everyday wear, ceremony, and repair.

Our goal is a size-inclusive, printable sewing pattern (SVG and PDF), editable source, cutting layouts, and documented construction. Pattern files are planned, not yet released.

Natural dye grimoire

Golden mageOnion skins · amber and gold
Moon wizardIndigo · midnight blue
Fire wizardMadder root · warm red
Forest wizardWalnut hulls · earthy brown

Scour linen first. Test dye recipes on swatches and record water, fiber weight, time, pH, mordant, and wash/lightfastness. Cellulose fibers may need tannin and alum preparation; indigo uses a different vat process. Actual shades vary.

Herbal blessing

  1. Earth: choose flax and dye plants with care.
  2. Water: wash the finished garment; optionally use a gentle, well-strained rosemary or lavender infusion after a patch test.
  3. Air: dry completely in moving air.
  4. Fire: speak an intention by candlelight, keeping fabric well away from flame.
  5. Spirit: tuck a removable dried-herb sachet into a dedicated pocket.

Keep concentrated essential oils off clothing and skin; they can irritate, stain, and pose risks to pets. Blessing is a personal symbolic practice, not a medical treatment.
