Universal Brain Browser

Your browsing history should become your own searchable, portable memory—not a trail owned by advertisers.

The vision

Turn visits, searches, notes, and research sessions into a personal knowledge timeline. Ask Operator what you read, how ideas connect, and where you left off.

Remember

Import existing browser history and index new visits with dates, titles, URLs, and optional page snapshots.

Understand

Full-text and semantic search, project associations, notes, and AI summaries.

Resume

Restore relevant tabs, context, and research sessions across devices.

Own

Open-source, local-first SQLite storage, optional end-to-end encrypted server sync, exports, deletion controls, and private-site exclusions.

Build plan

  1. Firefox/Chromium extension and explicit opt-in capture.
  2. History import and searchable timeline in 3DVR Operator.
  3. Local full-text indexing and optional semantic retrieval.
  4. Encrypted cross-device sync and session restore.
  5. Mobile integration and user-controlled knowledge graph.

Privacy principles

Never capture passwords, payment forms, private windows, or sensitive content by default. Keep page snapshots optional, allow per-site blocking and one-click deletion, and do not sell browsing data.

First milestone

A working browser-history importer and timeline search, backed by local storage and accessible from Operator. This is a proposal, not a claim of implemented functionality.
