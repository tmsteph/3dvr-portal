  • Basic interaction only observes events already delivered to this page.
  • Device look + motion attaches automatically where allowed. Browsers such as iOS Safari that require an explicit permission gesture still need one tap.
  • Camera motion is enabled only after you press the button. Video is processed locally at low resolution for frame-to-frame motion energy; frames are not uploaded or stored.
  • Audio is never requested.
  • Closing/reloading the page stops the active camera stream. You can also stop it here at any time.
  • This experiment does not send sensor values to a server.
