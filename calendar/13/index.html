Year

Tap a day to see its normal Gregorian date.

Model: this follows the International Fixed Calendar pattern: Sol is the 7th month, inserted between June and July. Every month begins on Sunday. Year Day follows December 28; Leap Day is inserted after June 28 in leap years. These special days have no weekday, so the 52-week rhythm never shifts.
